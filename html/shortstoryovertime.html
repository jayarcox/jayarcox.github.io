Overtime

by
J.R. Cox

You don’t actually hear the sound of your own breathing. I guess you hear it, but it isn’t something you pay attention to. Even when you can’t hear anything else, you just tune it out. Or, I do, rather. I guess I can’t speak for everyone. But even right now, I’m only hearing it because I’m thinking about it. Honestly, I more often get distracted by the sound of the blood pumping through my ears. I like that sound better anyway.

I listen to that sound a lot while I’m working because I work a lot outside. I actually love it out here. I used to talk to people when I went back to the surface. They said that it was frightening. It is frightening, but I think it’s the kind of frightening that God is. You’re scared of it, but you also kind of depend on it. Or, I do, rather. It gives me this sense of… something. You start to get kind of numb when you’re out here a while.

I’ve been out here a long time. I’m really good at what I do. Not much water actually comes from Earth. In fact there’s probably not enough there for the people that live there. So they have contractors come out here to the belt to find ice in the rocks and ship it back to the planets. You make lots of money doing it, because a lot of people need that water, but nobody likes coming out here. It can be kind of dangerous I guess.

There’s an art to it. You come out of the space station and float to the asteroids. It’s pretty far so it takes hours. You’re tethered to the station so you can get back, but I usually take it off to work. They say you’re not supposed to, but you really can’t work with it, so I doubt too many guys actually keep it on.

Then you start working. You hop from rock to rock, and you have these straps with magnets so you can attach yourself to it. You can’t really stay on them any other way because of the gravity. You start drilling in and you’d push yourself right off the rock.

I’d probably never come back in if it weren’t for the gravity making you weak and all. The human body isn’t designed for it. Some are better than others though. You really have to get used to. On the planets all you do is in relation to the ground. Seriously, most people can’t even function, even pour a cup of coffee without using the ground as a reference. Out here there’s no ground. That’s the art.

There’s only the two of you. You and the rock. And you can’t think of it as the ground because now you’re even. Like on Earth when you fall the ground wins. Out here, sometimes the rocks fall on you, and bounce off you as if you where the ground. At least that’s how it’ll go if you do it right.

That’s why it’s an art. Most guys aren’t that good at it, but I’ve been out here a while. I bring in a lot of water because I spend so much time working. Most guys out here work for big companies, but I don’t do that any more. They limit you too much.

For example, they only let you work for fifteen hour shifts before you come in. That’s crazy because you hardly find any thing in fifteen hours, let alone haul it in. Then you have to take twelve hours off, and by that time someone else has brought in your haul. They say you lose it if you work too much out here, but that’s just nonsense. Anybody with half a brain does just fine.

I’m not saying I’m not a little different. You spend so much time by yourself in the dark you’re going to forget a little bit about being with people. That doesn’t make you crazy though. I’m aware of it. I just don’t care.

I stay out here for days. And I bring in tons of water. Not really tons, but probably enough to supply a whole family for a week. And I do that every shift because I work for days at a time.

I bring food with me in my suit, which you’re also not allowed to do when you work for one of the companies. They don’t like you to have anything but what you’ll need for the job, which just isn’t practical. A lot of guys get funny out here. It’s a dangerous enough. If you take off that tether you’re pretty vulnerable. It doesn’t take a lot to just boot someone into space. So you need some deterrent.

I used to carry a serrated knife, which could have been perfect for cutting into a guy’s lifeline or poking a little hole in his suit. Everybody has one though, so it doesn’t really give you an advantage. I know it seems grim, but you can’t mess around out here. When I used to go back in, the guys would laugh about how it’s the old west out here. No law except the law you make. There’s just no way to regulate. I guess I can’t argue with that, but out here is far more brutal. There’s no surviving even a little mistake. You can’t take even one chance.

That knife did make me feel better because people would see how much I was hauling in and try to follow me. It was hard at first, like anything you aren’t used to, but morality is different out here. People aren’t different from the rocks. It’s all the same stuff ultimately. I like to think that the Earth used to be just like me. It was a rock that was better than the other rocks so all the other rocks just never affected it much. I’m just a rock too, and I guess I’m pretty good at it myself.

Finally I realized that the only way to stop guys from following me, was to start carrying this sword. I know it’s kind of silly, and you can tell they didn’t want to let me keep it on me, but it works great. You can see it from a ways away. Now they know not to mess with me. Except Jerry over there. He never gets too close, so I don’t think he knows that I know he’s there. I don’t know his real name of course, so I named him Jerry.

I like names that rhyme with bear because then I can nickname him Jer-bear. I think it’s funny.

This sword was a great investment though because it works really well out here. You can’t use a gun of course, and a harpoon or a bow and arrow won’t have enough force to do anything because you fly back just as much as it flies forward. I was going to make a spear for more reach, but that’s too long. Like I was saying earlier the only thing out here is you and the rocks.

When you need to fight a guy you have to grab on to him. You have limited range of movement in your suit, so a sword gives you enough maneuverability to still get into his suit up close. I’m an artist of that too.

It takes experience. I don’t think most guys think about it too much. That’s because they spend too much time thinking about the planets and when they can get back into gravity and stuff. Then they get in a scuffle and two things ruin them. They forget that I’m the ground, and they try to fight like there’s someplace they’re going to wrestle me down to. The other thing they mess up, is they think it’s going to happen slow.

It takes a long time to do anything in space. For example, Jer-bear is floating to me. He’s been on his way here for, like, twenty minutes. I won’t even jump towards him for another twenty minutes.

Then he thinks he’s going to have a good twenty minutes to wrestle around to my lifeline and cut me loose from this juicy rock, so he can drag it back himself. Things happen slow in space, but when you land on something, like a planet or a rock, you move fast in relation to the ground. When I land on him, he’s the ground. I’ll have my sword in him before he realizes I grabbed onto him.

Look at him. Waving at me. That means he wants to talk or something. We have these hand signals out here, so you can communicate, but I forgot most of them. Communicating doesn’t mean much to me anymore. He’ll just tell me he doesn’t want my rock. He just wants me to come in because I’ve been out here for 56 hours. I know because a guy came out at 26 hours, and another one at 48 hours.

That’s exactly what I would say if I were stealing a haul from me. If I wanted me to believe what I was saying, I’d say, “I want your rock, not your life.”

Something like that. I’d be really good at stealing hauls from guys, but I do better just finding my own so it’s not worth it.

He keeps waving like an idiot. Showing me his hands so I know he’s not armed. That’s fairly clever. He has a red suit too. That’s great. I bet this guy really draws them in because that’s a doctor’s suit.

If I were dumb like most of the guys out here, I’d believe him. They aren’t dumb because he tricked them though. They’re dumb because they don’t like it out here. They miss the gravity, and having this giant ground that will hold them and keep them safe. They don’t realize that you can be your own ground like me. That’s why they’re dumb. And they probably float out to give him a big hug because they want him to be a doctor as much as old Jer-bear wants them to think he’s a doctor.

Not me. I better go out and meet him. I have a lot of respect for Jer-bear though. That’s a good racquet. The problem is, he isn’t well protected. I have an armored suit. That just makes sense out here because you already can’t move too well, it doesn’t weigh anything and I have these big leather coats that hide where all of my life support tubes and things are. It just makes sense.

He’s so vulnerable, and he doesn’t even realize it. Now I’m in range to get a clear message. Should I listen to it?

I think so. I know that communicating is no good to a loner like me, but it could provide me with good information I can use. I won’t believe anything he says, but it may give me insight.

Here’s what I mean with my ears. I can hear my pulse in them. Pounding. I guess because I’m anticipating hearing his message. Nothing new. 79 hours? I guess my clock doesn’t go past 54. That makes sense I guess, but I haven’t eaten in a while. That’s actually good news because I think I can go for another two meals or so. I could add another two rocks to my stringer.

That’s exactly what I mean with the good information I get. I’m going to miss you Jer-bear. I miss Gar-bear from earlier, but I don’t so much miss Lar-bear. Gar-bear was firm. He talked like he was going to reprimand me for the “wanton cruelty” and what not. It was almost like he was actually talking to me and not just lying.

Lar-bear was totally different. So disingenuous. That’s a ten dollar word. I’m going to say that to Jer-bear right now. This guys a hoot. 79 hours is record I guess. He’s not saying it that way, but I figured it out.

It makes me sad that I have to go back in at all. I forgot that I hadn’t killed anybody before this shift. They’ll be really angry. They’ll probably want to not pay for my rocks, and not let me go out anymore. That’s nonsense. That’s not going to benefit anyone.

It’s funny how I forgot that. You forget things if you work for 79 hours. I took breaks though. I slept some. Ate some. I’m kind of on a break right now. I don’t know why that’s funny to me. I guess they don’t have breaks to kill people in most jobs.

There goes Jer-bear. I knew it wouldn’t be long. This is the first one I actually watched. I’m not going to watch another one. It’s disgusting. Like a peep in a microwave.

I must be in a good mood. Things just keep cracking me up. Getting back is a lot faster because you can crawl on your life line. I wonder what will happen when I get back.

I want to tell them the truth, but if I were a crazy murderer, I would say it was self-defense. That’s what I mean about how communication is useless. If I don’t say anything, they won’t like that any better.

I’ll worry about that when I get back. I wonder how long I can go out here. I guess until my air runs out. I wonder how much is left. They don’t tell you how much they put in because they want you to come back every fifteen hours.

That’s why I was thinking about my breath earlier. That’s right. Funny how you forget things out here. This little red light started blinking. That’s a perfect example. This light comes on an hour or so ago, and I’m still breathing well enough. I could just be tired from cutting Jer-bear’s suit and now hauling myself back on this line, and I haven’t eaten a lot for the last three days. You can’t even communicate with a little red light out here.

Now it’s stopping. Like I can trust it now. I bet I can keep going like this for another 24 hours. Just got to get back to my rock.

It’s a long way. I got to take a nap when I get back there.

Now I can’t stop listening to the sound of my breath. That’s really funny.
