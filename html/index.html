J.R. COX

Performer - StoryTeller - Artist

Biography

I'm a professional screen and voice actor. My voice work for companies ranging from Door Dash and Freshly to Shriners Hospitals and the Nature Conservancy can be heard on television and radio stations around the country, and my roles in the award-winning shorts Thorn, Thank You for Coming and Shadow of the Mountain have been featured in a variety of film festivals.

Originally from Alaska, I got my degree in film from Montana State University before moving to Los Angeles to begin my career. I worked in numerous roles in front of and behind the camera there before moving to Vermont, where I have continued to pursue my passion for film making with both narrative and non-fiction productions.

In addition to film and audio work, drawing and other visual arts have always been one of my favorite ways to express my creativity. Most of my portfolio is comprised of character designs, but more recently drawing landscapes, architecture, and figures from life have been capturing my attention and time more and more.

More About Me

I live in the New North End of Burlington, Vermont with my wife and two daughters. As a parent, I’m particularly passionate about ensuring Vermont has a bright future, which is why I orient my time toward supporting the people, organizations, and companies that uplift our community and state. I value diversity, sustainability, and forward-thinking.

What I'm Working on Now

One project I have in development is a puzzle adventure book that revolves around challenges that can be set up at playgrounds. It combines concepts from video games and escape rooms to provide an outlet for active, instrumental play outdoors for young people who are outgrowing free play but don't have interest in organized sports.
