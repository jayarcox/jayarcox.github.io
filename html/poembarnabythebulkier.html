Barnaby the Bulkier

by
J.R. Cox

Barnaby the Bulkier was strong and fierce in deed,
When his horse ran over much, he carried the poor steed.
Long of hair and long of beard, and wide of chest and bone,
He traveled o’er the countryside, a-questing for a throne.

Came Barnaby upon a land scourg’d by torrid flames
The cause of which was mystery, or so the story claims.
Thus Barnaby set down his horse and sought to douse the fire,
So people would be grateful and supply his heart’s desire.

He dug a trench from mountain stream to forest as it burn’d,
He toil’d hard but did not tire, or have you not learn’d?
Barnaby had great big arms, his back was broad and stout,
Bulkiness was in his name, it’s what this tale’s about.

The water pour’d down to the trees, til empty was the stream,
But so persistent was the heat that water was but steam.
The branches did get damper, but the trunks were burning too,
Plus underbrush and animals; the water wouldn’t do.
So Barnaby look’d to the earth and with a mighty heave,
Dug his arms so deep below, it muss’d his gilded sleeve,
Would you believe he lifted then and upset so much ground,
It rain’d back down like so much hail and landed all around?

Hurricanes were in his haunches, thunder in his thighs,
How could you still doubt the strength of such a bulky guy?
The dirt fell down onto the blaze and choked it were it could,
But so surrounding were the flames, it did not quench the wood.

Sighing, Barnaby then cough’d for it was smokey still,
But this gave him a grand idea to run back up the hill.
He ran and ran up to the peak, and never did he slow,
Such was that man’s fortitude, by now you ought to know.

It’s not that hard a concept, you should understand by now,
Barnaby was really strong and big you stupid cow!
His lungs were strong and big enough to breath in so much air,
That when he blew it out again it smothered out the flare.

Now Barnaby the Bulkier is famous and renowned,
Through virtues of his bulkiness a throne he fin’ly found.
His size and strength are clear to see, this poem’s made it plain,
It baffles me that you got here without that in your brain.
